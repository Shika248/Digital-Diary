📔 Digital Diary

A modern and interactive web-based digital diary designed to help users record daily thoughts, memories, moods, and personal reflections in an organized and visually appealing way.

✨ Features
📝 Create & Save Diary Entries – Write and save personal thoughts and memories.
📅 Calendar View – View diary entries by date and easily navigate through different days.
🕒 Timeline View – Browse previous memories in chronological order.
😊 Mood Tracking – Associate entries with moods such as Calm, Joyful, Inspired, Grateful, Reflective, Anxious, and Tired.
🔥 Writing Streak – Track consecutive days of journaling.
🏷️ Tags & Search – Organize entries using tags and search through memories, thoughts, and places.
📊 Journal Insights – View writing statistics, emotional patterns, word count, reading volume, and writing habits.
📸 Photo Support – Add photos to diary entries.
🎙️ Voice Dictation – Support for writing entries using voice input.
💡 Daily Inspiration – Get inspiration while creating an entry.
🎨 Theme Selection – Choose between different visual themes.
📍 Location Support – Add a location to diary entries.
⭐ Favorite Entries – Mark important memories for quick access.
✏️ Edit & Delete – Manage previously created entries.
🖥️ Main Sections
Timeline

Displays diary entries chronologically with their date, time, mood, location, tags, and word count.

Calendar

Provides a monthly overview of journaling activity and highlights dates containing diary entries.

Insights

Provides an overview of:

Current writing streak
Best streak
Total memories
Words written
Reading volume
Emotional resonance
Writing rhythm

🎯 Objective

The goal of Digital Diary is to provide a simple and engaging platform for users to document their everyday experiences, track emotions, maintain journaling habits, and reflect on personal growth.
